# Jaiwa widhyaawa
## sailaya

sailaya jiiwayee eekakayayi. saila patalaya
harahaa dhrawya gaman karayi.

- nyashtiya paalanaya karayi
- mayitokondriyaa shakthiya sapayayi

Q: saila patalayee kaaryaya kumakdha?
A: dhrawya aethulu wiima haa pitawiima paalanaya
   kiriimayi.

Mixture: jalaya (water) and shaaka.
